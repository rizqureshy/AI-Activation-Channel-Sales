Rhythm & the Grand Final

The weekly rhythm

  • Mon–Thu — optional activities, 15–20 minutes a day; they build toward Friday.
  • Friday — mandatory activity; submit in the channel.
  • AI Drop-In · optional support sessions — Mon & Thu 11:00–11:45 (tentative): Q&A, activity support, capstone and PBR help.

The Grand Final

  • Your entry — Week 4's Full PBR, submitted Friday, Aug 28. No separate deliverable.
  • Judged on — story clarity, insight quality, lifecycle alignment, executive readiness, AI craft.
  • The stage — nominations Wed Sep 9 · judging Thu–Fri · the winner presents at the All Hands, Mon Sep 21.
Data Rule — applies to every activity
🔒 All activities can be completed using sample scenarios, synthetic data, or publicly available information. If you plan on using anything proprietary — Equinix information, internal-only documents, data, customer information, or PII — restrict yourself to internal approved tools only.
